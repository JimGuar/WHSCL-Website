How the league works

Practice. Compete. Connect.

Practice

Every team practices two hours a week at the same day, time, and gym. Athletes learn knot tying and belaying, then build technique, movement, and bouldering skills alongside their teammates.

Compete

Monthly top rope and bouldering competitions run in four divisions from 5.9 through 5.12 and up, so beginners and experienced climbers compete against people at their own level. Competing is optional.

Connect

High School Hangouts run monthly and are free for every Wisconsin high schooler, in the league or not. Add clinics, team culture, and a season that ends with the whole league in one gym.

Is this for me?

You do not need to climb yet.

Any current high school student in Wisconsin can join. Climbing experience is not required, competing is not required, and every team teaches the basics from scratch.

What a season includes

Weekly team practice
Two hours, same day and gym each week, led by your coach.
Skills instruction
Knot tying, belaying, technique and movement, and bouldering.
Competitions
Monthly top rope and bouldering events across four divisions, plus State Finals.
High School Hangouts
Free monthly league events with themes, snacks, and climbing.
Clinics
Optional sessions for athletes who want to go deeper into advanced technique.

What it costs

$25 to join the league.

The annual league fee is paid once per season when an athlete signs the Code of Conduct. Competitions are $20 each. Practice access depends on your team’s facility — ask your coach about membership or day-pass options.
